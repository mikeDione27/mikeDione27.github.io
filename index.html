Michel Dione

PhD candidate specializing in Distributed Acoustic Sensing (DAS) and Deep Learning for spatio-temporal signal analysis. My research focuses on designing efficient Transformer-based architectures and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models for large-scale acoustic monitoring of infrastructures and environments. My work aims to address high-dimensionality challenges in DAS data through feature engineering, attention mechanisms, and lightweight architectures.

Keywords:

  • Deep Learning for Time-Series & Spatio-Temporal Data
  • Transformers & Vision Transformers
  • Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)
  • Anomaly Detection
  • Smart Infrastructure Monitoring
  • Robust detection under noise and domain shifts
